# LUP_

LUP – YOUR PERSONAL READING COMPANION
FOR A BETTER READING EXPERIENCE
ABOUT APP
SMALL DESCRIPTION

LUP is a smart reading assistant designed to convert anything you scan or paste into clear, human-sounding audio. With advanced OCR technology, users can scan images, extract clean text, and instantly listen to it in multiple languages and adjustable speaking speeds.

The app supports text pasting for quick reading, making it ideal for students, professionals, and anyone who prefers listening over reading.
LUP automatically maintains a full history of scanned pages and recently played items, allowing users to easily revisit their previous work.

With a modern interface and powerful text-to-speech engine, LUP offers a fast, smooth, and distraction-free reading experience.
